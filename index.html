Sessions

A personalised space for you.

Each 60–90-minute session is personalized around what you are experiencing. Most sessions are held online via Zoom or Google Meet, with in-person appointments available in Setúbal, Portugal.

After a gentle conversation about your needs, I combine Reiki, ThetaHealing® practices, intuitive guidance and a channeled meditation as appropriate. We close with reflections and integration guidance, and you are welcome to return whenever you need support—there is no set programme.
