About Carol Moura

Healing, guided by intuition and care.

I’m Caroline, an energy healer and intuitive practitioner committed to creating a grounded, welcoming space where you can pause, reconnect with yourself and restore a greater sense of balance. My approach is compassionate, attentive and guided by the understanding that every person’s experience is unique.

I work with practices including Reiki, ThetaHealing® and sound healing, sometimes supported by crystals, a pendulum or other energetic tools. However, the heart of my work is not the tools themselves, but presence, intention and the ability to listen deeply to what you need in that moment. Together, we identify and release the energetic and emotional blocks that may be preventing you from living a lighter, more fulfilling life. My purpose is to support you in reconnecting with your inner wisdom, clarity and personal power.
